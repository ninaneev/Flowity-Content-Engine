## Cadence & Batching System

**One batching session per week (~2–3 hrs).** In it:
- One teaching anchor post (the week's main LinkedIn/X post + newsletter edition)
- 5–6 short daily posts spun from the same anchor — one idea, seven ways

**Newsletter:** 1x/week, same day as teaching anchor.
**Daily posts:** scheduled, never live. Buffer or LinkedIn/X native scheduler.
**Worst-week floor:** queue carries you. Nothing needs to be written in real time.

## Signature Move

Two words. No period drama. The reader just saw something — you name it for them. Trains recognition over time.
